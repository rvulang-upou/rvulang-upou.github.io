ABOUT MITOLOKAL

Wear the Myth, be Legendary

At Mitolokal, we bring the wonders of Philippine mythology into everyday fashion. Inspired by the legendary creatures and folklore passed down through generations, we transform our rich cultural heritage into our designs. These retro-inspired T-shirts celebrate the stories that make us proud to be Filipinos.

To ensure quality and comfort, our shirts are made from 100% cotton, offering a soft and comfortable feel that you can enjoy every day. At Mitolokal, we value your comfort while creating designs that celebrate our cultural identity.

Mitolokal invites you to wear something with a story to tell, a design to cherish, and a new story to discover. Through every shirt, we hope to keep our myths and legends alive, inspiring a new generation to appreciate the richness of Philippine folklore.
