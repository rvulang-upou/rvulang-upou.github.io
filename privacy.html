5. Philippine Privacy rights

Website privacy rights in the Philippines are primarily governed by Republic Act No. 10173, known as the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (DPA), and overseen by the National Privacy Commission (NPC)

Rights of Website Users (Data Subjects)

When visiting or submitting data to our website, Filipino users hold eight core rights under the National Privacy Commission (NPC):

  • Right to be informed: Know whether your personal data is being processed, the purpose, and how it is handled.
  • Right of access: Request and obtain a copy of the personal data a website holds about you.
  • Right to rectification: Dispute and correct inaccuracies or errors in your personal data.
  • Right to erasure or blocking: Suspend, withdraw, or order the removal/destruction of your data from a website's filing system under valid grounds.
  • Right to object: Opt-out of data processing, including commercial communications, direct marketing, or profiling.
  • Right to data portability: Obtain your personal data in an electronic, structured, and commonly used format.
  • Right to be indemnified: Claim compensation for damages sustained due to unlawful, unauthorized, or inaccurate processing.
  • Right to file a complaint: Report privacy violations or unresolved data breaches directly to the National Privacy Commission (NPC).
